101 Revamp Proposal

The purpose of this is to restructure 101 so that it better fits within a four-day instructional week while providing students with stronger foundations in visual literacy, design analysis, and replication. Before asking students to produce original work, I'd recommend the course give them opportunities to observe, deconstruct , and recreate existing designs. This progression will help students develop a deeper understanding of design principles and decision-making before applying those principles to their own original work.

Week 1: Introduction to Computers & Graphic Design

This week should introduce graphic design and the design process. Students should be able to understand how to do all five steps in the design process and setup an illustrator document.

    Rework: Students are given a scenario that they must do a small client write-up on to define a project. Based on the scenario, students must state: who the client is, what the project is, what the objective of the project is, who the primary audience is, what message the audience needs to know, what deliverable(s) are being produced, what limitations and requirements they have, and what the outcome should be.
